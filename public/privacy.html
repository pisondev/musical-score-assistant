Privacy

Musical Score Assistant · last changed 3 October 2026

Musical Score Assistant shows and plays piano scores. You can use it without an account. Signing in with Google lets the app keep your favourites and settings on every device. This page says what is kept, why, where, and how to remove it.

Who runs it

The site is a personal, non-commercial project run by the owner of the repository pisondev/musical-score-assistant on GitHub. Questions and requests can be sent there as an issue.

What is kept when you sign in

  • From your Google account: your name, your email address, and the address of your profile picture. Nothing else is asked of Google, and the app never sees your password.
  • What the app remembers for you: your favourite songs, the songs you opened last with the left hand you chose, and your display settings.
  • When you first signed in and when you last used the site.

Without an account, the same settings stay in your browser only and never reach the server.

Why

Only to give you your favourites and settings on every device you sign in on. There is no advertising, no tracking, and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared with anybody.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • msa_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days; msa_login lasts ten minutes while you sign in. Both are needed for signing in and are not used for anything else.
  • The app keeps your settings in the storage of your browser. Installed as an app, it also keeps copies of the app and of the songs you opened, so that they work without a connection.

Where it is kept

Accounts are kept on the server of the site, in Indonesia. A copy of them is made every day and kept for 14 days in storage of Cloudflare, which also carries the traffic of the site. Google handles the sign-in. Like any web server, the site writes logs with IP addresses, which are kept for a short time to keep the site running.

How long, and how to remove it

Your account is kept until you delete it. Open the account menu (your picture at the top right) and choose Delete account: your account and everything kept for it are removed at once, and from the daily copies within 14 days. You can also ask for a copy of what is kept about you through an issue on GitHub.

Changes

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. What is kept will not be used for anything other than what this page describes.
