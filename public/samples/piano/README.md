# Piano samples

These recordings are a subset of the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm, a sampled
Yamaha C5 grand, released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) licence.

The files are the MP3 conversions distributed with [Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io/). One note
is sampled every three semitones from A1 to C6; the player pitch-shifts the notes in between.
